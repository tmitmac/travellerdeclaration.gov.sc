Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about using the traveller declaration, travel history and declaring belongings.

Does each member of my family need a declaration?

Yes. Prepare a separate declaration for each traveller, including children and infants. A parent or guardian may complete the answers and sign for a child.

How long does the declaration take?

Allow around 10–15 minutes if your passport, travel details and information about your luggage are ready. Complex itineraries or items may take longer.

What counts as a transit country?

Include countries or territories where you stopped or changed transport during the past 30 days, even if you did not leave the airport or port. The form lets you identify a transit-only stop.

I do not know whether an item needs to be declared. What should I do?

When in doubt, answer Yes to the relevant question and describe the item. Border officers can assess its status. You should never guess that a potentially relevant item is exempt.

Do I need to declare packaged food?

Yes, use the food question even for sealed, dried or packaged products. A product being commercially packaged does not necessarily remove biosecurity risk.

Does a Yes answer mean my goods will be seized?

No. A Yes answer provides information for assessment; it does not automatically mean that something is prohibited or will be removed.

Can someone help me complete a declaration?

Yes. Another person can assist you. You remain responsible for providing accurate information, and a parent or guardian may sign for a child.

Can I change answers after printing a copy?

Yes. Reopen your draft if saved, make corrections and print the updated summary. You should not use an outdated declaration after your answers have changed.

Where is my information saved?

This version processes answers in your browser only. If you select Save draft, the data is stored in the browser on that device. It is not transmitted to a government server.

Can I submit the form online?

Not on this standalone website. The final action prepares a printable copy for review; it does not transmit or register an official declaration with border authorities.

What if I am arriving by sea?

Select Arriving by sea in the journey section. The form will ask for the vessel name, arrival port and overseas departure port instead of a flight number.

Will the form work offline?

Yes, the HTML, styles, icons and scripts are packaged locally. Opening the files in a browser or hosting them as static pages does not require any third-party service.

Need more information?

Read the preparation guide, Customs guidance and biosecurity guidance. If you have an unusual or urgent border situation, seek advice from the relevant Customs or border authority rather than relying only on a prepared copy.
