Section 4 of 7

Customs declarations

Answer each question about goods and belongings you are bringing.

Think about everything you are bringing

These questions cover goods in checked baggage, cabin bags and any items you carry on your person. You may need to provide further information during inspection.

Goods or gifts acquired overseas, including purchases for yourself or others? *
Include items obtained overseas even if they are used or were given to you.
Alcohol, tobacco, vaping or similar excisable products? *
Include items in all bags and purchases from duty-free shops.
Large amounts of cash, traveller’s cheques or other negotiable monetary instruments? *
Be ready to give the amount, currency and ownership.
Commercial goods, trade samples, stock for sale or specialist work equipment? *
Include tools or goods that will be used in a business.
Controlled medicines, unusually large quantities of medication or medical substances? *
Keep supporting prescriptions or medical letters if relevant.
Weapons, ammunition, hazardous items, wildlife products or other potentially restricted goods? *
Include items that may require a permit, authorisation or inspection.
Baggage, parcels or items you are carrying for another person? *
Declare items even if someone else owns them.
Goods or personal effects arriving separately by freight, courier or mail? *
Include items shipped before or after your arrival.

Details of the goods you declared

Describe the item, quantity, approximate value or amount, and who owns it. List more than one item if needed.

Section 5 of 7

Biosecurity declarations

Answer questions about food, natural items, outdoor equipment and recent activities.

When in doubt, answer Yes

Declaring something does not automatically mean it is prohibited. Officers can assess items that may pose a biosecurity risk.

Any food or drink products? *
Include fresh, dried, cooked, preserved, factory-sealed and packaged products, including snacks and ingredients.
Animal products or items of animal origin? *
Include meat, dairy, honey, eggs, hides, skins, feathers, bones and shells.
Live animals, insects, animal specimens or biological samples? *
Include pets and items for scientific or educational purposes.
Plants, seeds, flowers, fruit, wood, straw or other plant products? *
Include untreated wooden souvenirs and natural decorations.
Soil, mud, sand, compost or items with visible dirt or plant material? *
Include items contaminated with seeds or animal material.
Used hiking boots, camping equipment, sports or outdoor recreation gear? *
Include tents, bicycles, walking poles, golf equipment and protective clothing.
Fishing, hunting or freshwater equipment used overseas? *
Include rods, waders, dive gear, boats and items used in rivers, lakes or wetlands.
Visits to farms, contact with livestock or use of farm equipment recently? *
Include farm stays, animal handling, agricultural properties and animal markets.
Contact with wild animals, animal habitats or wildlife collection during your trip? *
Include hunting, animal handling or collection of biological materials.
Any other items that may carry seeds, pests, soil, untreated natural material or biosecurity risk? *
If you are unsure about an item, declare it.
