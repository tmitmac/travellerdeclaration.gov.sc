What you need to begin

Have these details handy before completing the declaration. The questions cover one traveller and their accompanied or unaccompanied belongings, including checked and cabin baggage.

Passport and identity

Full name as shown in the passport, date of birth, nationality, passport number, issuing country and expiry date.

Journey details

Arrival date, flight number or vessel name, departure location, arrival port, and the first place you will stay.

Recent travel history

Countries and territories visited or transited through during the 30 days before your arrival, including dates where known.

What is in your bags

Purchases, gifts, food, plants, animal products, outdoor gear, medicines, currency and any items carried for others.

Who completes a declaration?

Every traveller preparing to enter Square Country should have their own declaration, including citizens returning home, overseas visitors, children and infants. A parent, guardian or other trusted person may assist someone in completing their answers.

For children, the parent or guardian should check what is inside their luggage and sign the final confirmation on their behalf.

When should you prepare it?

Prepare the information once your journey and arrival details are known, and review it if your flight or vessel details change. This site does not set an official submission deadline or provide border-agency transmission; it allows you to prepare and print a complete copy.

Before you begin

Travellers may still be asked questions or requested to show belongings at the border. The declaration is not a visa, passport or permission to enter Square Country.
